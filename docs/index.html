Flow

The clock only counts focus that actually happened.

A calm Pomodoro companion for Windows that floats quietly above your work, structures the day into focus flows, and walks away from the machine with you — five minutes of inactivity and the focus pauses and rewinds. Never a phantom minute.

What it does

Honest focus
Away time is rewound, not counted. No phantom minutes, no nagging.
Streaks
A day counts when focus was really logged; one missed day a week can be healed.
Weekly report
Focused vs planned for seven days, best day, longest flow — computed locally.
Site blocking
Listed sites go unreachable only while a focus is actually running (Windows).
Sound
Four chime voices and synthesized rain, brown noise, or café ambience.
Share status
Copy one honest line: “Focusing until 4:32 PM · Flow 2 of 4”.
Stays put
Remembers its window, launches at login if you want, hides to the tray.
